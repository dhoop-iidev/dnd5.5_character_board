# House rules & non-SRD register

Everything the playboard uses that is **not** plain SRD 5.2.1 (2024 rules). Only `confirmed` entries may be implemented.
`pending` entries need the user's (and DM's) decision first. Pure SRD for now (user decision 2026-10-25).

| # | Topic | Decision | Source | Status | Confirmed on |
|---|-------|----------|--------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Weapon Mastery from two classes (e.g. Barbarian 1 / Fighter 1) | **Pending.** The SRD (p. 25 "Multiclassing") lists special rules only for Extra Attack, Spellcasting and AC formulas; nothing about Weapon Mastery, and no online consensus was found (2026-10-25). Options: (a) the counts add up (two separate features, each granting "N kinds of weapons": what the app does today, 3 + 2 = 5); (b) only the higher count applies, as for Extra Attack. Default until decided: (a). | SRD 5.2.1 p. 25; rulings-classes.md | pending | — |
| 2 | Channel Divinity from Cleric and Paladin | **Pending.** The 2014 rule ("getting the feature again doesn't give you an additional use") was not kept in the SRD 5.2.1 multiclassing text. Options: (a) two separate pools, each with its class's uses and effects (what the app does today); (b) one pool with the higher count, usable for both classes' effects. Default until decided: (a). | SRD 5.2.1 p. 25, p. 37, p. 54 | pending | — |
| 3 | Custom background | **Confirmed.** Besides the 4 SRD backgrounds, a player may build a background with the same parts as the SRD ones (p. 83): three different abilities for the +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1, one Origin feat among the SRD's (p. 87), two different skill proficiencies, one tool proficiency (SRD tools, p. 93) and 50 GP of equipment (option B of every SRD background); an optional name of the player's own. Chosen over importing the 2024 Player's Handbook or the fan wiki's (Unearthed Arcana playtest) backgrounds, which are not open content. `model/customBackground.ts`, checks `background-custom-*`. | SRD 5.2.1 p. 83, p. 87, p. 93; user decision | confirmed | 2026-10-29 |
| 4 | Custom feats | **Confirmed.** The player may enter feats from other books (2024 Player's Handbook or others the DM allows) in the character: name, category (Origin, General, Fighting Style, Epic Boon), book and page, prerequisites (level, one ability at a minimum score, free text), the +1 Ability Score Increase choices, Repeatable, a summary in the player's own words, and optional modifiers the sheet applies. They are then offered wherever a feat of their category is chosen (a level's feat instead of the Ability Score Improvement, an Origin feat, a Fighting Style) and checked like SRD feats. Nothing from those books ships with the app: the entries live only in the character file. `model/customFeats.ts`, `Character.customFeats`. | user decision | confirmed | 2026-10-29 |
| 5 | Custom subclasses | **Confirmed.** The player may enter subclasses from other books (the 2024 Player's Handbook has three more per class than the SRD) in the character: class, name, book and page, a description and features by class level in the player's own words. Each feature may carry what the sheet can use: uses per Short or Long Rest (a number, the Proficiency Bonus, an ability modifier or the class level, with steps by class level), the part of the turn it takes (a card in the turn box), a list of named options to choose from (maneuvers…) and modifiers; the subclass may give always-prepared spells by class level (SRD spells, spellcasting classes only). Tier 3 (2026-10-29): a subclass may give its class **spellcasting** with every number from the player's book (ability, class spell list, cantrips and prepared spells by class level, the slot table by class level, and its share of the Multiclass Spellcaster table as class level ÷ N rounded up or down: the SRD p. 25 counts only full and half casters, so the app assumes nothing and a check asks for it when another class casts spells); a feature may **create a weapon** (built like a catalog weapon, with its own name, damage, type and range, proficiency and mastery boxes; not counted against the hands); a Druid feature may **change Wild Shape** (maximum CR, Temporary Hit Points, a minimum AC, as formulas; the highest of the table and the formula applies); a **creature from the player's book** can be added in the Companions pane (subclass companion…), its AC, Hit Points, Proficiency Bonus and attack numbers being formulas that follow the character (`model/formula.ts`). Wild Shape still doesn't replace the main sheet's numbers (known gap), only the in-play card. A subclass can be exported to its own file and imported into another character. Nothing from those books ships with the app. `model/customSubclasses.ts`, `Character.customSubclasses`. | user decision | confirmed | 2026-10-29 |
| 6 | Custom species | **Confirmed.** The player may enter species from other books (Tabaxi from *Volo's Guide to Monsters*…) in the character: name, book and page, creature type, one size or two to choose from, Speed, a description and traits in the player's own words, each from a character level (1 for most). Each trait may carry what the sheet can use: Darkvision range, Climb/Swim/Fly Speed (feet, or equal to the Speed), skill proficiencies, damage resistances, uses per Short or Long Rest (a number, the Proficiency Bonus or an ability modifier), the part of the turn it takes, and modifiers. **No ability score increases**: under the 2024 rules they come from the background (SRD 5.2.1 p. 20), so an older species' increases are dropped, as the 2024 Player's Handbook does for older species. A species can be exported to its own file and imported into another character. Nothing from those books ships with the app. `model/customSpecies.ts`, `Character.customSpecies`. | user decision | confirmed | 2026-10-29 |
