# House rules & non-SRD register

Everything the playboard uses that is **not** plain SRD 5.2.1 (2024 rules). Only `confirmed` entries may be implemented.
`pending` entries need the user's (and DM's) decision first. Pure SRD for now (user decision 2026-10-25).

| 1 | Weapon Mastery from two classes (e.g. Barbarian 1 / Fighter 1) | **Pending.** The SRD (p. 25 "Multiclassing") lists special rules only for Extra Attack, Spellcasting and AC formulas; nothing about Weapon Mastery, and no online consensus was found (2026-10-25). Options: (a) the counts add up (two separate features, each granting "N kinds of weapons": what the app does today, 3 + 2 = 5); (b) only the higher count applies, as for Extra Attack. Options: (a) two separate pools, each with its class's uses and effects (what the app does today); (b) one pool with the higher count, usable for both classes' effects. Default until decided: (a). | SRD 5.2.1 p. 25, p. 37, p. 54 | pending | — |
