# Um, actually… — re-check of 2026-10-25 (after the fixes)

Scope: the 14 Meaningful findings of `um_actually-2026-10-25.md`, the rewritten engine (`src/engine/sheet/*`, `checks/*`, `rests.ts`, `hitPoints.ts`), the new effects data (`src/data/custom/effects/*.json`, every class feature to level 12 and beyond) and the twelve level-10 examples. Two reviewers; details, pages and reproductions in `parts/recheck-engine.md` and `parts/recheck-examples.md`. The findings they raised were then fixed as noted below.

## The 14 findings of the morning review

All fixed and reproduced through the engine: Fighter 11/20 attacks (3/4), Monk Martial Arts (Dex, Martial Arts die, Light martial weapons only), Weapon Mastery counts for Rogue/Paladin/Ranger (2) with a build check, Epic Boon (30) and capstone (25) caps, Fighter "Strength or Dexterity" prerequisite, any feat you qualify for at an ASI level, Human Versatile and Skillful (extra feat and skill, choices in the General pane), Divine Order / Primal Order, per-class maximum spell level, the four Circle of the Land lists chosen per Long Rest, Font of Inspiration, armor Stealth Disadvantage, the 15 conditions as effects, Temporary HP cleared by a Long Rest, Boon of Spell Recall's Spellcasting prerequisite. Of the 35 Minor items, 19 are fixed (rests at 0 HP, dead characters, massive damage, death saves with Exhaustion, Exhaustion 6, Resourceful, passive ±5, species effects and per-rest uses, Innate Sorcery, Tireless, repeatable feats, Fighting Style category, Expertise count, spellbook list, attunement copies and carried items, subclass noted at level 1, choice counts for Magic Initiate / Metamagic / invocations, mastery count and proficiency).

## New findings of the re-check, and what was done

- **N1 (Meaningful, fixed):** the cap was applied once at the end, so ASIs that overflowed 20 before an Epic Boon or Primal Champion were kept (Fighter 20 with three +2 Str and a boon → 24). Now two phases: everything but boons and capstones is capped at 20 (p. 20, p. 87), then the boon (+1, max 30, p. 88) and the capstone (+4, max 25, p. 30 / p. 52) apply.
- **N2 (Meaningful, fixed):** a Shield added +2 without Shield training. "You gain the Armor Class benefit of a Shield only if you have training with it" (p. 92): the Shield now contributes 0 and the warning says so (the old message claimed Disadvantage and no spells, which p. 92 reserves for armor).
- **Examples M1 (Meaningful, fixed):** the level 3 and 5 lineage/legacy spells of Elves and Tieflings (p. 84, p. 86) were missing. `spells.byLevel` (by character level) now grants them; spells granted by a species trait or a feat are prepared whatever the class and no longer count against a class's prepared or cantrip numbers.
- Minor, fixed: Rage's Advantage and Resistances only while raging (toggle); Martial Arts off while wielding a non-Monk weapon (p. 50); multiclass Rogue Thieves' Tools / Bard instrument reach the proficiency list; Petrified grants Immunity to the Poisoned condition, not Poison damage (p. 186); Innate Sorcery's +1 DC applies to Sorcerer spells only (`spell.dc.sorcerer`); Blessed Warrior / Druidic Warrior add two cantrips to the class's count (p. 54, p. 58); the ability increase of an extra feat (Versatile…) is checked (+1 among the feat's abilities, or none); Large Form's text says "from character level 5".
- Minor, still open (recorded for later): Magical Secrets accepts any number of off-list spells (p. 33 allows only the new picks); Barbarian masteries accept ranged weapons (p. 29 melee only); Thrown weapons have no ranged line; Loading weapons get the full attack count; a missing Fighting Style choice isn't flagged; Mage Armor can't be an active-effect AC formula; three invocation prerequisites truncated to "a Warlock Cantrip" and prerequisites other than "Level N+" unchecked; `spellcasting.ritual` false in the data (ruling D4, the engine doesn't read it); typicalDcs French strings empty; French terms "Appel de familier" / "Appel de destrier" / "outre-monde" / "Magie de pacte" not yet aligned; Brutal Strike 2d10 at 17, Divine/Primal Strike 2d8 at 14 (text-only "improved" features); Pact of the Blade grants no proficiency with the bonded weapon and applies to every weapon in hand; Pact of the Chain forms allowed for any Warlock; Relentless Rage modelled as 1/Short Rest instead of an escalating DC; Boon of Spell Recall accepted for a Warlock (prerequisite "Spellcasting Feature"); seven examples use rolled-style ability totals (legal only as rolled scores, p. 21); English identity text in nine French-language examples.
- Nitpicks still open: CLAUDE.md "half the Hit Dice" (SRD: all), a few page citations (SRC_TEMP 19 → 18, character.ts "p. 15" → 187, Wild Shape temp HP p. 43), the "null for the Net" comment.

## Examples

All twelve level-10 examples: `validate()` returns no warning of any severity, and every number recomputed by hand (abilities, HP, AC, initiative, speed, saves, skills, attacks, DCs, slots, counts, resources) matches `compute()`; the lineage spells above were the only disagreement.

## Verdict

The numbers are right for every class at level 10, for Monks, Fighters 11+, masteries, Humans, Fighter dips, multiclass spell levels, Land Druids, ticked conditions and armor Stealth. The remaining gaps are rarely used rules or level 13+ features, listed above and in the "Needs engine" text-only entries.
