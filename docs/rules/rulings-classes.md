# Rulings — classes and features (how the SRD text is read)

| # | Question | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Do the Temporary Hit Points of Wild Shape vanish when the druid reverts? (user question 2026-10-25: "the temporal hp stays, is it normal?") | **Yes, they stay.** Wild Shape grants ordinary Temporary Hit Points ("you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to your Druid level", p. 43) and the general rule is the only one that ends them: "Temporary Hit Points last until they're depleted or you finish a Long Rest" (p. 18). Nothing in Wild Shape or in "Rules While Shape-Shifted" removes them on reverting; the 2024 design replaced the 2014 separate beast hit-point pool with this Temporary HP pool precisely so that it carries over. Online guides (rpgbot "2024 Wild Shape", D&D Beyond druid forum) read it the same way; no Sage Advice ruling says otherwise. The app keeps them and says so in the Companions pane. | SRD 5.2.1 p. 43 (FR p. 44), p. 18 "Temporary Hit Points" (FR p. 19) |
| C2 | Invisible Stalker Initiative "+7 (22)" | Not a misprint: the stalker has the Invisible condition, which gives Advantage on Initiative ("Surprise", p. 184); the Monster Manual convention adds 5 to the Initiative score for Advantage. See rulings-data.md D1. | p. 184, p. 254 |
| C3 | Fighter multiclass prerequisite "Strength or Dexterity" | One of the two at 13+ satisfies the rule (p. 24 "primary ability"; the Fighter's Core Traits list "Strength or Dexterity", p. 47). Monk, Paladin and Ranger list two abilities with "and": both are needed. `ClassDef.primaryAbilityAny`. | p. 24, p. 47 |
| C4 | A feat other than Ability Score Improvement at an ASI level | Any feat whose prerequisites are met, of any category: "You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify" (every class) and "If you're instructed to choose a feat and no category is specified, you can choose from any category" (p. 87). The Epic Boon feature at 19 says the same. | p. 87, e.g. p. 48 |
| C5 | Spell levels a multiclass caster may prepare | Per class, from that class's own table row ("as if you were a single-classed member of that class"), even when the shared Multiclass Spellcaster slots go higher. | p. 25 |
| C6 | Font of Inspiration | From Bard level 5 Bardic Inspiration returns on a Short Rest as well (resource override). | p. 32 |
| C7 | Weapon Mastery count for Rogue, Paladin, Ranger | Two kinds of weapons (feature text; no table column). | p. 54, 58, 62 |
| C8 | Nick (p. 90): which weapon must have Nick, the one making the first attack or the one making the Light extra attack? | **The weapon making the extra attack** (user decision 2026-10-29, reading 1): a mastery property is used when you attack with that weapon. With a Dagger (Nick) and a Shortsword, the Dagger's extra attack after a Shortsword attack is inside the Attack action; the Shortsword's extra attack after a Dagger attack takes the Bonus Action. No official ruling found; forum readings differ. Two of a stack held one in each hand (`ItemInstance.pair`, two Daggers) count as two different Light weapons for p. 89. `engine/turn.ts` `lightExtraAttacks`. | SRD 5.2.1 p. 89 "Light", p. 90 "Nick"; [D&D Beyond forum](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/dungeons-dragons-discussion/rules-game-mechanics/213010-someone-explain-nick?comment=28) |
